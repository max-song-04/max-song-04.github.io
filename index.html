About Me

I'm a fourth-year mechanical engineering student at the University of Texas at Austin, focused on using engineering design to solve real problems. I work as a Student Assistant at Texas Inventionworks, UT's makerspace, and as a Structures Engineer for the Texas Rocket Engineering Lab — building composite rocket structures, running the machine shop trainings, and designing whatever I can talk my way into.

Outside of that, I'm into tech and stocks, play volleyball with the UT men's club team, and spend a fair amount of time trying new food around Austin. I like learning things I don't know yet, which is most of why this site exists. Feel free to reach out below.
