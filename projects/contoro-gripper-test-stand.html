Gripper Validation Test Stand — Contoro Robotics

From January to August 2026 I worked as a Mechanical Engineering Co-op at Contoro Robotics, an early-stage startup building robotic systems for warehouse and logistics work. Robots there ship in fast, iterative generations, which meant I spent as much time building the tools that build and test the robots as I did on the robots themselves.

End-of-Line Test Stand

Every gripper assembly needed to be validated before it went on a robot — checking suction, pneumatics, and electrical integration against a known-good reference before it left the bench. I designed and built a mobile test stand for this: a rolling cart carrying the compressor, valves, wiring, and fixturing needed to power up and cycle a full gripper assembly on demand, plus the software to drive the validation sequence. Rather than every technician improvising a bench setup, the stand turned gripper validation into a repeatable five-minute check.

Cantilevered Gripper Support Stand

The gripper needed to hang off the edge of a bench, fully suspended, so it could be rotated between two working positions during test and assembly. I designed a cantilevered shaft-and-bearing support stand for this — sizing the shaft and pillow-block bearings against the gripper’s weight and center of mass, and running hand calculations for bending, torsion, and deflection to confirm the design before committing to hardware. The final design carried the gripper with a large structural margin while staying simple enough to manufacture in-house on a tight timeline.

Manufacturing and Field Commissioning

Beyond the test tooling, I worked hands-on across manufacturing: releasing assembly drawings and BOMs for gripper and robot subsystems, designing quick stabilization and mounting brackets to solve fit issues as they came up on the floor, and helping build and wire full-scale warehouse robots — camera towers, junction boxes, and gripper end-effectors included. I also traveled to a customer fulfillment center to commission newly built robots and retrofit earlier units up to the current hardware revision, which meant seeing the whole pipeline through from CAD to a robot running in a live warehouse.

Skills: Mechanical Design, DFM/DFA, SolidWorks, Pneumatics, Test Fixture Design, Manufacturing & BOM
